# LAB-14 - Incident reconstruction capstone

**120 minute capstone. Follows U11. Units: U11, U12, U13, U14.**

## 0. Safety and isolation

- Work only inside the CPIA range. Never run these commands on a production or personal device.
- Malware samples come from an inert, course-built set. Do not execute them. Every sample in
  this course is static analysis only.
- Take a snapshot of the range before you start if you intend to change anything on a host.
- No real client, patient, student or colleague data appears in any dataset here. If you find
  what looks like real personal data in a public corpus, stop, do not copy it, and tell the
  trainer.

## What the capstone asks

You are the analyst on call. A host on the range has been reported as slow and the service desk suspects malware. You have every dataset from LAB-01 to LAB-12 and 120 minutes. Produce a timeline, an incident report, and a 20 minute defence of your finding.

## Timetable

| Segment | Minutes |
|---|---:|
| Brief and evidence handover | 20 |
| Reconstruction | 70 |
| Defence, per learner | 30 |

## What must be in the report

| Section | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Summary | Three sentences, one finding, no tool names |
| Timeline | One time zone, every event sourced, at least 12 rows |
| Evidence | Every claim cites a file, packet or log line |
| Confidence | Each finding marked strong, moderate or weak, with a reason |
| Limits | Two things you could not determine and why |
| Actions | Containment, eradication, recovery, in that order |

## Marking bands

| Band | Descriptor | Looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Distinction | Defensible | Every claim evidenced, limits stated, actions sequenced |
| Merit | Sound | Evidence mostly cited, one unsupported claim, sound actions |
| Pass | Adequate | Timeline correct, report readable, some claims unsourced |
| Refer | Not yet | Timeline wrong or missing, findings asserted without evidence |
